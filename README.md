# 🏋️‍♂️ CoCoach — AI-Powered Virtual Coach for Athletes

CoCoach is an **AI-driven virtual coaching platform** that converts a **camera and smartwatch into a real-time personal coach**.  
It combines **computer vision, wearable intelligence, and adaptive AI** to deliver **personalized, corrective, and safe athletic training**.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Most athletes train using:
- Static workout plans
- Generic fitness apps
- Limited access to professional coaches

This leads to:
- Incorrect posture
- Overtraining or undertraining
- Higher injury risk
- Lack of personalized feedback

**CoCoach solves this by acting like a real coach — watching movement, understanding the body, and adapting training in real time.**

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

CoCoach introduces two core intelligence modules:

### 🔹 ADFi — Adaptive Fitness Intelligence  
### 🔹 COMO — Motion Correction Engine  

Together, they form a **multimodal virtual coach**.
---

## 🧠 ADFi — Adaptive Fitness Intelligence

**ADFi is the brain of CoCoach.**

It dynamically adapts training plans based on **physiological and performance signals** collected from wearables and training history.

### What ADFi Analyzes
- Heart Rate & HRV
- Fatigue & recovery levels
- Sleep quality
- Training consistency
- Load vs readiness

### What ADFi Adapts
- Workout intensity
- Rest intervals
- Exercise selection
- Recovery recommendations
- Injury prevention mode

**In simple terms:**  
> ADFi decides *how hard an athlete should train today*, not based on a fixed plan, but on their body condition.

---

## 🎥 COMO — Motion Correction Engine

**COMO is the eyes of CoCoach.**

It uses **computer vision and pose estimation** to analyze athlete movement through the device camera.

### What COMO Tracks
- Joint angles
- Alignment and balance
- Movement symmetry
- Posture and form

### What COMO Delivers
- Real-time posture correction
- Injury-risk alerts
- Technique optimization feedback
- Visual correction via avatar
- Voice/text coaching cues

**In simple terms:**  
> COMO watches *how you move* and corrects you like a coach standing in front of you.

---

## 🔗 How ADFi & COMO Work Together
Together, they convert **any camera + smartwatch into a smart virtual coach**.

---

## 🧍 Virtual Coach (Avatar Layer)

CoCoach integrates a **3D avatar-based coach** to:
- Demonstrate correct form
- Visually compare athlete movement vs ideal movement
- Improve engagement and understanding

This makes coaching:
- Intuitive
- Human-like
- Immersive
